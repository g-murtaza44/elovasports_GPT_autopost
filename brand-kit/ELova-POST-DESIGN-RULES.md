# Elova Sports — Visual Identity & Post Design Rules

**Status:** Standing production rules  
**Reference:** User-supplied Elova header and footer screenshots (2026-11-01)  
**Applies to:** Every Elova Sports social post, unless the user explicitly approves a different format.

## 1. Non-negotiable layout

Reproduce the supplied header/footer system. Do not reinterpret it, replace it with a generic sports template, or omit either section.

### Header
- Use a wide, darkened sports photograph as the background (stadium roof, ground, crowd, arena, track, court, pool, etc., matched to the story).
- Place the Elova logo at the upper-left, with the lime mark, white ELOVA wordmark and small lime SPORTS subline.
- Place a small, thin lime/white outlined, rounded category label at the upper-right (e.g. WORLD RECORD · SWIMMING).
- Keep the header compact, cinematic and restrained. The photograph remains visible beneath a dark green/black overlay.

### Main story panel
- Use the deep-green panel as shown in the supplied footer reference.
- Add a compact context line near the top: flags where relevant, teams/athletes/event and location, with one key word highlighted in lime.
- Use a large, bold, condensed, uppercase headline. Keep the hierarchy and line breaks close to the reference.
- Highlight only the key phrase/word with a solid lime rectangle and dark text. Do not turn every word into an accent.
- The story must be visual: use the actual athlete(s), action, venue, ground/court/pool and/or crowd atmosphere as the main image content. **Typography-led does not mean text-only.**
- Keep the bottom social icons centered and aligned like the reference (Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn). Use clean white circular icon treatments.
- Preserve the reference's spacing, compactness, alignment and clear safe margins. Do not let tags, profile bubbles or UI overlays obscure the headline.

## 2. Vintage / editorial treatment

Match the supplied reference's dark, muted, cinematic sports-publication look:
- Deep forest green / near-black shadows; restrained lime highlights.
- Slightly desaturated, low-key photographic treatment with subtle grain/texture.
- Strong contrast and dramatic stadium lighting, but retain visible detail in the photograph.
- Editorial, mature, premium and atmospheric — not glossy neon, cartoonish, futuristic, or esports-style.
- Avoid bright blue, purple, gradients that dominate the design, excessive glow, heavy 3D effects, and decorative clutter.
- Do not apply an artificial “old paper” sepia filter. “Vintage” here means restrained, timeless sports-editorial atmosphere, consistent with the reference.

## 3. Brand colours and type

Use the existing Elova brand kit:
- Deep green background: `#07160E`
- Mid green panels: `#123322`
- Lime highlight / brand mark: `#C8F031`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Dark-on-light SPORTS accent: `#3E7A12`
- **No blue.**

Typography:
- Archivo Black: ELOVA wordmark and major headline / big numbers.
- Barlow Condensed 700/800: SPORTS, category tags, context line, dates and supporting labels.
- Headlines should be uppercase, tightly set, bold and readable at phone size.

## 4. Sports photography and kit accuracy

- Prefer verified photographs from the exact match, series, tournament or event.
- If a generated/illustrative visual is used, do not present it as an authentic documentary photograph.
- When depicting a named athlete, do not invent facial likeness or claim the image is an actual match moment.
- **Kit must match the actual jersey worn in the specific match/series/event.** Verify the correct season, tournament, colour, design, sponsors and visible details from a reliable image source before using it.
- Never substitute a generic national/team kit or a kit from another series.
- Include the real venue and crowd/playing environment where relevant. Do not use unrelated stadium imagery as if it were the event venue.

## 5. Accuracy and editorial checks

- Verify every score, statistic, spelling, name, date, venue, competition and record against reliable sources before designing.
- Do not ask an image model to render critical statistics or text. Add all typography and numbers separately after image creation.
- Do not invent scoreboard details, overs, jersey sponsors, match events, player positions or records.
- Ensure the headline says exactly what the verified story supports. Distinguish a match performance, a series award and a world record.
- Keep the caption consistent with the artwork. Do not add unsupported claims.

## 6. Production workflow

1. Research and verify the story from primary/credible sources.
2. Identify the exact event/match and find a suitable, rights-cleared or otherwise approved visual reference.
3. Verify athlete identity and the exact kit for that event.
4. Build the image composition around the header / story panel / footer reference.
5. Add all text, stats, labels and social icons as controlled design elements—not generated image text.
6. Check phone-size readability, margins, contrast, spelling and visual consistency.
7. Show the finished preview for approval before committing or publishing.
8. Save paired assets using the repository naming contract:
   - `images/YYYY-MM-DD-N.png`
   - `captions/YYYY-MM-DD-N.txt`

## 7. Reject / do not publish if

- Header or footer is missing or materially redesigned.
- The result is a text-only poster with no meaningful sports imagery.
- Players, venue, crowd or event context are unrelated or misleading.
- The kit is wrong, generic or unverified.
- Any score/stat/name/date is inaccurate or rendered incorrectly.
- The design is bright blue, neon, cluttered, cartoonish or unlike the supplied visual reference.
- The final image has not been reviewed at actual social-post size.

## 8. Source-of-truth priority

1. User's latest explicit instruction.
2. User-supplied Elova reference screenshots and approved feed examples.
3. This document.
4. Existing repository brand kit.

If a future request conflicts with these rules, ask one concise clarification rather than silently changing the established identity.
